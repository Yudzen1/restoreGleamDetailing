Services

Interior Detailing

Thorough cabin cleaning for seats, carpets, trim and hard-to-reach areas. Tell us about stains, pet hair or odors when you request an estimate.

Exterior Detailing

Careful exterior cleaning for paint, wheels and visible trim. Ask about wash and wax or combine it with interior detailing.

Paint Correction

Polishing to improve gloss and reduce visible swirls and light scratches. The right process depends on your paint condition; deep damage may need body-shop repair.

Buff

Targeted polishing to improve gloss and visible paint defects. We recommend a process after assessing the finish.

Polishing

Light polishing / DA polishing to enhance gloss and remove light defects.

Touch Up Paint

Chip touch-ups to improve appearance and help protect exposed areas.

Remove Scratches

Scratch, scuff and swirl removal where possible depending on depth and clear coat condition.

Ceramic Coating

Ceramic protection for long-lasting gloss and easier washing.

Wax

Wax protection for shine and water beading.

Headlight Polishing

Headlight restoration and polishing for better clarity and appearance.

Boat Detailing

Boat wash, polishing, oxidation removal, gel coat shine restoration and wax protection.

Before you book

Do you come to my location?

Yes, we offer mobile detailing in Chicago and the North Shore. Send your location so we can confirm the service area and workspace needs.

How much does detailing cost?

Your estimate depends on the vehicle size, condition and services. Text your model, location and photos for a quote.

Can you remove every scratch?

Polishing can improve many light scratches and swirls. Deeper damage may need touch-up paint or body-shop repair. Photos help us assess the next step.

Do you clean pet hair and stains?

Tell us about pet hair, stains and odors when requesting interior detailing. Results and any additional work depend on the materials and condition.

What if the weather changes?

Mobile work depends on suitable conditions and workspace. Contact us to confirm the plan before your appointment if rain or cold is expected.

Do you detail boats?

Yes. Ask about boat washing, polishing, oxidation removal and wax protection. Send photos, boat size and its location for an estimate.
